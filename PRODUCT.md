# Mars Pathfinder AI

A research instrument for exploring real Mars data and comparing terrain-aware walking routes.

Audience: Space Apps judges, students, and scientifically curious explorers.
Primary task: play actual forward-facing NASA rover imagery with a shared route on
the Mars map, see the recorded pose advance, and inspect real re-routing/U-turn
guidance. Real footage is the default; a 3D simulation is secondary. Global Mars
exploration, terrain routing, model analysis and evidence remain separate modes.

Use scene: desktop mission control, with responsive exploration on smaller screens.
Visual reference: NASA Eyes, without copying NASA branding or assets.
Voice: concise scientific instrument labels; every computed claim is sourced and measurable.

Truth constraints: no flight qualification, no guaranteed EVA safety, no fake model metrics,
no fabricated obstacles, no unverified camera placement, no claim of two trained models
when the orbital component is deterministic. Camera/orbital fusion requires independent controls.

Github authorship: never add an AI coauthor or contributor identity. Disclose AI tool usage in
the contest documentation separately, as required by the event rules.
